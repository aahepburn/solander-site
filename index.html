Mac apps for document privacy

Redact PDFs and clean Office documents on your Mac.

Solander is two apps for preparing documents before you share them. Solander PDF redacts PDFs, scans and images, and shows the metadata and hidden content each file contains. Solander Desk removes hidden content from Word, Excel and LibreOffice documents and keeps them editable. Both check the file after exporting it, and neither sends your documents anywhere.

The apps

Solander PDF

Redaction and metadata review for PDFs, scans and images

Use it when the document you share will be a PDF.

  • Finds names, email addresses, phone numbers, ID numbers and dates for you to review, including on scanned pages.
  • Lists the metadata and hidden content in the original: author and title fields, earlier saved versions, attachments, comments, form data, scripts and location data in images.
  • Exports a new PDF built from page images, so none of that is carried over, and reads it again to check every redaction.
  • With a licence: signed reports of what was removed and checked, batch processing and a command-line tool.

Solander Desk

Hidden content in Word, Excel and LibreOffice documents

Use it when the document has to stay editable.

  • Finds tracked changes, comments, author names, hidden sheets, rows and columns, cached data, links to other files and cropped-out parts of images.
  • Removes what you choose and keeps the document editable in Word, Excel or LibreOffice.
  • Checks the exported file again from disk before handing it to you.
  • With a licence: replaces names with placeholders before you paste a document into an AI assistant, and puts them back in the reply. Batch cleaning.

Privacy

Your documents stay on your Mac.

Both apps run in Apple’s App Sandbox without network access, so macOS blocks the app that opens your files from making any connection. There is no account, telemetry, analytics or crash reporting.

You can check this on your own copy:
codesign -d --entitlements - /Applications/Solander.app
codesign -d --entitlements - "/Applications/Solander Desk.app"

In each app, two small helper processes use the network: one checks for updates, and one confirms your licence about once a week. Neither receives a document or anything found in one. The privacy notice lists exactly what they send.

Limits

What the checks cover

After exporting, each app checks that what you chose to remove is no longer in the file, and that the file is sound. The checks do not show that everything sensitive was found. Detection can miss things, so you review every file. Neither app describes a document as anonymous, compliant or safe, and pseudonymized text is still personal data. The apps are tools and a record of what they did, not legal advice.

Questions

Which app do I need?

If the result will be a PDF — a disclosure, a published document, a scan or a screenshot — use Solander PDF. If the file has to stay a Word, Excel or LibreOffice document, for example a draft contract or a workbook you send to a client, use Solander Desk. If you do both, the Suite covers both apps.

Do my documents go anywhere?

No. In both apps, the app that opens them has no network access, enforced by macOS. Update checks and licence confirmation run in separate helper processes that never receive document data.

Why two apps rather than one?

The two jobs work differently. Solander PDF rebuilds every page as an image, so nothing is left underneath, and the result is no longer editable. Solander Desk keeps a Word, Excel or LibreOffice file editable, so it works inside the file’s own structure and checks every part of it.

Do I have to buy anything to try them?

No. Each app is free for one document at a time, with no time limit and no account. A licence adds working on many documents at once, signed reports and automation, and in Solander Desk, pseudonymizing for AI.

I already own Solander PDF. Do I pay full price for the Suite?

No. You pay the difference, and your new Suite licence works in both apps. Your existing licence keeps working too. You will need the licence ID from your licence email and the address it was sent to.

Is there a subscription?

No. A licence is bought once and includes future versions. About once a week a helper process confirms the purchase has not been refunded; if it cannot reach us, the licensed features keep working for 30 days, and a Mac that is never online can be activated offline.

What happens if I ask for a refund?

The licence is deactivated and the licensed features stop. Free mode keeps working as before, and everything you have already produced is untouched. See Refunds.
