Private by design

Your documents never leave your Mac.

That is enforced by macOS, not promised by us. Both apps run in Apple’s App Sandbox without network access, so the operating system refuses any outbound connection from the app that opens your files. No account, no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting.

Check it on your own copy:
codesign -d --entitlements - /Applications/Solander.app
codesign -d --entitlements - "/Applications/Solander Desk.app"

In each app, two small helper processes do use the network — one checks for updates, one confirms your licence about once a week. Neither ever receives a document or anything found in one. Exactly what they send.

Plain about their limits

What a passing check means — and what it does not.

It means what you chose to remove is gone, and the file you received is sound. It does not mean everything sensitive was found. Detection is best-effort and misses things, which is why you review every file, and why neither app ever calls a document anonymous, compliant, safe or clean. Pseudonymized text is still personal data. These are tools for the work and a record of what they did, not a substitute for judgement or legal advice.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do my documents go anywhere?

No. In both apps, the app that opens them has no network access, enforced by macOS. Update checks and licence confirmation run in separate helper processes that never receive document data.

Why two apps rather than one?

Because the two jobs pull in opposite directions. Solander PDF makes a document safe to release by rebuilding every page as an image, so nothing is left underneath — and the result is no longer editable. Solander Desk keeps a Word, Excel or LibreOffice file editable, so it has to work inside the file’s own structure and check every part of it. Each app does one of these well; the Suite gives you both.

Do I have to buy anything to try them?

No. Each app is free for one document at a time, with no time limit and no account: every finding shown, the export, and the check afterwards. A licence adds working on many documents at once, the exportable evidence, and automation — and in Solander Desk, pseudonymizing for AI.

I already own Solander PDF. Do I pay full price for the Suite?

No. You pay the difference, and your new Suite licence works in both apps. Your existing licence keeps working too. You will need the licence ID from your licence email and the address it was sent to.

Is there a subscription?

No. A licence is bought once and includes every future version. About once a week a helper process confirms the purchase has not been refunded; if it cannot reach us, the licensed features keep working for 30 days, and a Mac that is never online can be activated offline.

What happens if I ask for a refund?

The licence is deactivated and the licensed features stop. Free mode keeps working exactly as before, and everything you have already produced is untouched. See Refunds.
