Why Solander

Most redaction tools stop at drawing the box. Solander checks that it held.

Rewrites the page, not a box on top

Every page is rendered to an image, the redactions are painted in, and a new file is written from scratch. No text layer, metadata, annotations, attachments or earlier versions are carried across — there is nothing underneath to recover.

Checks its own work

After exporting, Solander reopens the file and reads it again. Anything still legible where a redaction was meant to be is reported, not quietly exported. You see this result whether or not you have a licence.

Says what it could not read

Text the OCR found but could not resolve is listed and marked on the page, so you know which parts no detector examined. Unreadable is reported as unreadable — never counted as clean.

Evidence you can hand over Licensed

A signed report in PDF and JSON: what was redacted and why, what was verified, and what the original file contained. A recipient can re-check it against the document without taking your word for it.

Private by design

Your documents never leave your Mac.

That is enforced by macOS, not promised by us. Solander runs in Apple’s App Sandbox without network access, so the operating system refuses any outbound connection from the app that opens your files. No account, no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting.

Check it on your own copy: codesign -d --entitlements - /Applications/Solander.app

Two small helper processes do use the network — one checks for updates, one confirms your licence about once a week. Neither ever receives a document or anything Solander found. Exactly what they send.

How it works

Three steps, and you decide every redaction.

  1. Open

    Drop in a PDF, a scan or an image. Solander lists what the file carries beneath the surface — metadata, attachments, annotations, scripts — and flags what it finds for you to review.

  2. Review

    Mark what has to go. Accept what Solander found, add anything it missed, and leave what should stay.

  3. Export and verify

    Solander writes a fresh file, re-reads it, and shows you the result — including anything it could not read.

Plain about its limits

What a passing check means — and what it does not.

It means no readable text remains inside the regions you redacted. It does not mean everything sensitive was found. Detection is best-effort and misses things, especially names, addresses and non-Latin text — which is why the review step exists, and why Solander never calls a document anonymous, compliant or safe. It is a tool for the work and a record of what it did, not a substitute for judgement or legal advice.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do my documents go anywhere?

No. The app that opens them has no network access, enforced by macOS. Update checks and licence confirmation run in separate helper processes that never receive document data.

Do I have to buy anything to try it?

No. Free mode has every detector and every language, and lets you open, review, redact, export and verify one document at a time — with no time limit and no account. A licence adds working on many documents at once, the exportable evidence, and automation.

Is there a subscription?

No. A licence is bought once and includes every future version. About once a week a helper process confirms the purchase has not been refunded; if it cannot reach us, the licensed features keep working for 30 days.

Can I use it on a Mac that is never online?

Yes, with offline activation. The app shows a short code, you enter it on a web page from your phone or another computer, and type the answer back. A first activation lasts 15 days and each renewal lasts 90.

What happens if I ask for a refund?

The licence is deactivated and the licensed features stop. Free mode keeps working exactly as before, and everything you have already produced is untouched. See Refunds.

Does it replace a person checking the document?

No. Detection misses things, especially names, addresses and non-Latin text. Solander is built to tell you where it is uncertain rather than hide it, but the review step exists because no detector finds everything.
