Why Solander Desk

Most “remove metadata” tools clear the author field. The rest of the file still talks.

Finds what the reader cannot see

Tracked changes and the text they deleted. Comments and who wrote them. Hidden and “very hidden” sheets, hidden rows and columns. Pivot caches that hold a full copy of the source data. Links to other workbooks, with values cached from them. The whole picture behind a cropped image. Template paths, document variables, revision IDs, and markup other software left behind.

Keeps the file editable

A Word file comes out as a Word file, a workbook as a workbook. You decide what happens to tracked changes — accept all or reject all, never guessed. When removing a hidden sheet would break a formula, that formula keeps its value, and you are shown exactly which cells changed.

Checks its own work

After writing, Solander Desk opens the new file from disk and checks it again: that it is structurally sound, that nothing you removed is anywhere inside it, and that what a reader sees is what it was. A file that fails is not handed to you.

Pseudonymize for AI, then restore Licensed

Replace names, organisations and identifiers with consistent placeholders across a set of files before you paste them into ChatGPT or Claude — then put the real values back into the answer. The mapping stays on your Mac, encrypted, and can be forgotten for good.

Private by design

Your documents never leave your Mac.

That is enforced by macOS, not promised by us. Solander Desk runs in Apple’s App Sandbox without network access, so the operating system refuses any outbound connection from the app that opens your files. No account, no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting.

Check it on your own copy: codesign -d --entitlements - "/Applications/Solander Desk.app"

Two small helper processes do use the network — one checks for updates, one confirms your licence about once a week. Neither ever receives a document, a name from one, or a placeholder mapping. Exactly what they send.

How it works

Three steps, and nothing is decided for you.

  1. Open

    Drop in a .docx, .xlsx, .odt or .ods file. Solander Desk lists what it carries, by risk, and shows you the hidden content itself if you ask.

  2. Decide

    Accept or reject tracked changes. Remove or keep hidden sheets; clear, unhide or keep hidden rows. Everything else removable goes by default.

  3. Export and verify

    Solander Desk writes a new file into a “Solander Exports” folder, never over the original, re-reads it, and shows you what it checked.

Plain about its limits

What it checks — and what it does not.

Verification checks that what you chose to remove is gone and that the file is sound. It does not prove every sensitive item was found, and it cannot promise how every version of every office app will display the file. Parts of a file it does not understand are removed and reported, never passed through unexamined. Pseudonymized is not anonymous: surrounding details can still identify people, and under the GDPR pseudonymized data is still personal data.

Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), and LibreOffice Writer and Calc (.odt, .ods), including macro-enabled files and templates, which come out as ordinary documents. Older .doc and .xls files, password-protected files, PowerPoint and Pages, Numbers and Keynote files are not cleaned: re-save them as one of the formats above.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does it change my original file?

Never. It writes a new file with the same name into a “Solander Exports” folder, and will not write into the folder the original is in, so the two are never mixed up.

What happens to tracked changes?

You choose, per file: accept all, or reject all. Either changes what the document says, so Solander Desk never picks for you, and it will not export until you have chosen.

Will the file still open in Word or Excel?

That is the whole point, and it is what we test hardest. Solander Desk rewrites only the parts it has to, leaves the rest byte for byte as it was, and checks the file’s structure after writing it.

How does pseudonymizing for AI work?

You give Solander Desk the names and terms to replace, and review what it finds. It replaces them with placeholders like (PERSON-1) consistently across every file in a project, and copies the text for you to paste. When the answer comes back, paste it into Restore and the real names go back in — even if the AI wrote the placeholder a little differently.

Do my documents go anywhere?

No. The app that opens them has no network access, enforced by macOS. The placeholder mapping is stored encrypted on your Mac, with its key in your Keychain, and never sent anywhere.
