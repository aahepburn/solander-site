Solander Desk

Remove hidden content from Word, Excel and LibreOffice documents.

Solander Desk finds tracked changes, comments, author names, hidden sheets and other content that a document contains but its reader cannot see. You choose what to remove, and the document stays editable. The exported file is checked again before it is handed to you.

Features

What Solander Desk does

Finds hidden content

Solander Desk finds tracked changes and the text they deleted, comments and their authors, hidden sheets, rows and columns, and pivot caches that hold a copy of the source data. It also finds links to other workbooks and the values cached from them, the full picture behind a cropped image, and template paths, document variables and revision IDs.

Keeps the document editable

The exported file has the same format as the original and opens in Word, Excel or LibreOffice as before. You decide what happens to tracked changes: accept all or reject all. When removing a hidden sheet would break a formula, that formula keeps its value, and you are shown exactly which cells changed.

Checks the exported file

After writing, Solander Desk opens the new file from disk and checks that it is structurally sound, that nothing you removed is left inside it, and that the visible content is unchanged. A file that fails the check is not handed to you.

Pseudonymize for AI, then restore Licensed

Replace names, organisations and identifiers with consistent placeholders across a set of files before you paste them into an AI assistant such as ChatGPT or Claude, then put the real values back into the reply. The mapping is stored encrypted on your Mac and can be deleted at any time.

Privacy

Your documents stay on your Mac.

Solander Desk runs in Apple’s App Sandbox without network access, so macOS blocks the app that opens your files from making any connection. There is no account, telemetry, analytics or crash reporting.

You can check this on your own copy: codesign -d --entitlements - "/Applications/Solander Desk.app"

Two small helper processes use the network: one checks for updates, and one confirms your licence about once a week. Neither receives a document, a name from one, or a placeholder mapping. The privacy notice lists exactly what they send.

How it works

  1. Open

    Open a .docx, .xlsx, .odt or .ods file. Solander Desk lists the hidden content it contains, ordered by risk, and can show you the content itself.

  2. Decide

    Accept or reject tracked changes. Remove or keep hidden sheets, and clear, unhide or keep hidden rows. Other hidden content is removed by default.

  3. Export and check

    Solander Desk writes a new file into a “Solander Exports” folder, never over the original, reads it again, and shows you what it checked.

Formats and limits

Supported files, and what the check covers

Solander Desk works with Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), and LibreOffice Writer and Calc (.odt, .ods), including macro-enabled files and templates, which are exported as ordinary documents. Older .doc and .xls files, password-protected files, PowerPoint, and Pages, Numbers and Keynote files are not supported; re-save them in one of the formats above.

The check confirms that what you chose to remove is gone and that the file is sound. It does not show that every sensitive item was found, and it cannot guarantee how every version of every office app will display the file. Parts of a file that Solander Desk does not recognise are removed and reported. Pseudonymized text is not anonymous: surrounding details can still identify people, and under the GDPR it is still personal data.

Questions

Does it change my original file?

No. It writes a new file with the same name into a “Solander Exports” folder, and never into the folder the original is in, so the two are not mixed up.

What happens to tracked changes?

You choose, per file: accept all, or reject all. Either one changes what the document says, so Solander Desk does not choose for you, and it will not export until you have chosen.

Will the file still open in Word or Excel?

Yes. Solander Desk rewrites only the parts it has to, leaves the rest of the file exactly as it was, and checks the file’s structure after writing it.

How does pseudonymizing for AI work?

You give Solander Desk the names and terms to replace, and review what it finds. It replaces them with placeholders like (PERSON-1) consistently across every file in a project, and copies the text for you to paste. When the reply comes back, paste it into Restore and the real names are put back, even if the AI wrote a placeholder slightly differently.

Do my documents go anywhere?

No. The app that opens them has no network access, enforced by macOS. The placeholder mapping is stored encrypted on your Mac, with its key in your Keychain, and never sent anywhere.
