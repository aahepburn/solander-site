Model card

The model, in the open

What it is

A small named-entity model that runs on the Mac, beside pattern detectors for ID numbers, and nothing that leaves the machine.

Trained on

Synthetic documents in Dutch, French and English in the forms public bodies and courts use. No customer document is ever in the training set.

Scored on

A held-out set, labelled by hand, published with the labels. Rerun it with the scoring code in the repository.

Known misses

Short names, names in images and scans the OCR cannot read, and names in scripts other than Latin. The app shows where it could not see.

The benchmark repository, the model card with version and date, and the first run are published with the 1.0 release.
