Features

What Solander PDF does

Finds what to redact

Solander PDF detects names, email addresses, phone numbers, ID numbers, dates and other personal information, in the text layer and on scanned pages. You review each finding, add your own redactions, and leave what should stay.

Shows the file’s metadata and hidden content

When you open a file, Solander PDF lists what it contains besides its visible pages: document information and XMP metadata, earlier saved versions, attachments, comments and annotations, form data, hidden layers, scripts, and location data in images.

Exports a new file

Each page is rendered to an image with the redactions applied, and a new PDF is written from those images. The text layer, metadata, attachments and earlier versions of the original are not carried over.

Checks the result

After exporting, Solander PDF reads the new file again and reports any text still legible inside a redaction. Text it could not read is listed and marked on the page, so you know which parts no detector examined.

Signed reports Licensed

A report in PDF and JSON records what was redacted and why, what was checked, and what the original file contained. It is signed and includes a hash of the exported file, so a recipient can confirm that the report belongs to that document.

Batches and automation Licensed

Redact many documents at once with names kept consistent across them, record a reason code for each redaction and export a redaction log, and use saved profiles, the command-line tool and watch folders.

Privacy

Your documents stay on your Mac.

Solander PDF runs in Apple’s App Sandbox without network access, so macOS blocks the app that opens your files from making any connection. There is no account, telemetry, analytics or crash reporting.

You can check this on your own copy: codesign -d --entitlements - /Applications/Solander.app

Two small helper processes use the network: one checks for updates, and one confirms your licence about once a week. Neither receives a document or anything found in one. The privacy notice lists exactly what they send.

How it works

  1. Open

    Open a PDF, a scan or an image. Solander PDF lists the file’s metadata and hidden content, and marks the personal information it finds.

  2. Review

    Accept or dismiss each finding, draw redactions for anything it missed, and leave what should stay.

  3. Export and check

    Solander PDF writes a new file, reads it again, and shows the result, including any text it could not read.

Limits

What the check covers

A passing check means no readable text remains inside the regions you redacted. It does not mean everything sensitive was found. Detection can miss things, especially names, addresses and non-Latin text, so you review every document. Solander PDF does not describe a document as anonymous, compliant or safe, and it is not legal advice.

Questions

Do my documents go anywhere?

No. The app that opens them has no network access, enforced by macOS. Update checks and licence confirmation run in separate helper processes that never receive document data.

Do I have to buy anything to try it?

No. Free mode has every detector and language, and lets you open, review, redact, export and check one document at a time, with no time limit and no account. A licence adds batches, signed reports and automation.

Is there a subscription?

No. A licence is bought once and includes future versions. About once a week a helper process confirms the purchase has not been refunded; if it cannot reach us, the licensed features keep working for 30 days.

Can I use it on a Mac that is never online?

Yes, with offline activation. The app shows a short code, you enter it on a web page from your phone or another computer, and type the answer back. A first activation lasts 15 days and each renewal lasts 90.

What happens if I ask for a refund?

The licence is deactivated and the licensed features stop. Free mode keeps working exactly as before, and everything you have already produced is untouched. See Refunds.

Does it replace a person checking the document?

No. Detection misses things, especially names, addresses and non-Latin text. Solander PDF shows you where it is uncertain, but every document still needs a person to review it.
